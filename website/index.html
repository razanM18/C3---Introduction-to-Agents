Why solar?

Reliable power, understood by you

Lebanon gets plenty of sun: roughly 5 to 5.5 peak-sun hours a day for planning on the coast and in the Bekaa. A well-sized system with a battery keeps your lights, fridge, internet and more running when the grid or the generator is off.

  • Fewer disruptions. Power cuts and generator dependence affect daily life less.
  • You see everything. Live data in the CedarSun Home app, and our team is alerted automatically if something is wrong.
  • Honest numbers. We state our assumptions, losses and limits in every proposal.
  • Safety first. Electrical work is done only by qualified CedarSun technicians.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is the site survey really free?

Yes. The survey and consultation are free and come with no obligation.

Do you publish your prices?

No. Every home is different, so every price is a written quotation in US dollars after the free site survey.

How long does installation take?

Typically 1 to 3 days once your quotation is approved. This is an estimate, not a guarantee.

Can I run air conditioners on solar?

Yes, with the right size. The Smart Home Hybrid covers one air conditioner for part of the day and the High-Demand Home is for several. The Essential Backup is not designed for air conditioners.

Which areas do you cover?

Beirut, Mount Lebanon (Metn, Keserwan, Baabda, Aley, Chouf), Jbeil, Batroun, Tripoli, Saida and Zahle. Other areas on request after review.

Who answers in the chat?

Zeina, CedarSun's AI solar assistant. She can answer questions, give a rough system size and book your free survey. Our human specialists follow up on every request.
