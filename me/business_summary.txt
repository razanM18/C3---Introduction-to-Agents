CEDARSUN ENERGY - BUSINESS SUMMARY
(CedarSun Energy is a fictional company created for a university assignment.)

WHO WE ARE
CedarSun Energy S.A.L. is a Lebanese residential solar company founded in 2019 and based in Sin el Fil, Beirut. We design, install and support rooftop solar panels, hybrid inverters and lithium (LFP) batteries for homes, villas and apartments. We serve Beirut, Mount Lebanon (Metn, Keserwan, Baabda, Aley, Chouf), Jbeil, Batroun, Tripoli, Saida and Zahle. We do not install commercial or industrial systems. MISSION AND WHAT MAKES US DIFFERENT
Our mission is to give Lebanese households reliable, understandable electricity from the sun, so power cuts and generator dependence disrupt daily life less. Our difference is honest, engineer-led sizing plus live monitoring: every customer gets a free, no-obligation site survey, a written load analysis showing how the design was calculated, and the CedarSun Home app (live generation, consumption, battery level and fault alerts).

SIZING METHOD (rough estimates only - use the estimate_system_size tool, never do the arithmetic yourself)
Daily energy kWh = sum(watts x hours) / 1000. Array kW = daily kWh / (sun hours x 0.80). Panels = array kW / 0.45 (450 W panels), rounded up. Battery nominal kWh = energy to store / (0.90 x 0.90), in 5.12 kWh modules; if the customer gives no backup target, plan the battery for 40 percent of daily energy. Inverter: assume 70 percent of connected appliance power runs at once, choose the smallest of 3, 5 or 8 kW that covers it, at least 5 kW if an air conditioner is included; above 8 kW is a Custom Design for an engineer.
Planning sun hours: coast (Beirut, Jbeil, Batroun, Tripoli, Saida) 5.0; Mount Lebanon 5.2; Bekaa (Zahle) 5.5; unknown area 5.0.
Always call results approximate and subject to the free site survey. Estimates ignore shading, roof orientation, season and dust.
